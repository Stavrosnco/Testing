[Verse 1]
[Ensemble narrator, rapid-fire theatrical rap, piano and strings]
Tristram burned and the old cathedral bled,
The hero who went under came back with a stone in his head,
Left a town full of ghosts and the scholar for dead,
Hung up in a gibbet while the little devils fed—
[Shrill goblin chant]
Rakanishu! Rakanishu! Bishibosh!
[Narrator]
Rogue Encampment, campfire glow,
Charsi at the anvil— (Hey, nice armor!) —sparks and hammer blows,
Akara says the last of the Horadrim's alive,
So a stranger walks to Tristram, cuts the cage, and he survives,
And the old man stretches out his bones and smiles,
Says the words that'll echo for a thousand miles—

[Chorus]
[Full ensemble, big Broadway hip-hop chorus]
Deckard Cain! (Deckard Cain!)

Stay awhile and listen! (Stay awhile and listen!)
Hello, my friend, you're gonna wanna hear this one,
This does not bode well for us, my friend—
But the story's never done! Deckard Cain!

[Verse 2]
[Old scholar voice, smug, quick wordy flow]
Now you drag in a sack from the Blood Moor field,
Red letters on the sword and the helmet and the shield,
"Unidentified"? Lad, that's a riddle, not a curse,
And Akara wants a scroll's worth of gold from your purse?
Put away the Tome, put the Scrolls back on the shelf,
Bring it to the last of the Horadrim himself.
Back in Tristram it was one hundred gold a piece,
But you cut me from that cage, so for you, the charges cease.
(And if the Rogues had fetched me first?)
Two hundred gold apiece— could've been worse!
